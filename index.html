Privacy Policy — Heaven & Hell Cascade

Last updated: 25 November 2026

Heaven & Hell Cascade is a single-player puzzle game published as uk.marlsoft.heavenhellcascade on Google Play by Marlsoft (United Kingdom).

The short version

The game does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data. It has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics, no crash-reporting service and no third-party SDKs of any kind. It does not request the Android INTERNET permission, so it is not technically capable of sending anything off the device.

What is stored on your device

The game keeps its own progress and settings — current level, score, unlocked relics, sound on/off, and the state of a level in progress — using the web storage that belongs to the app's own WebView. This never leaves the device and is not readable by other apps.

It is deleted when you uninstall the game or clear its data in Android Settings. If Android's own backup service is enabled on your device, Android may include this local file in its device backup; that backup is handled entirely by Google's Android backup system, not by us.

Permissions

The app declares one permission: VIBRATE, used for haptic feedback when you swap tiles. It has no access to your location, camera, microphone, contacts, files, photos or any other personal information.

Children

The game collects no data from anyone, including children. There are no ads, no purchases and no social features.

Changes

If a later version ever adds a feature that needs data (for example an online leaderboard), this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the Google Play Data safety section will be updated with it.
